# LOLO image library

Presentation defaults for event covers and gifts. Files live in `public/images/library/`.
The guest page resolves them at render time and does not write them to the database.

License for every photo: **Pexels License** ([https://www.pexels.com/license/](https://www.pexels.com/license/)).
Free commercial use, no watermark, attribution not required. Photos were downloaded from the Pexels image CDN and stored in the repo. They are not hotlinked.

`blue-stack` is credited to Pixabay on its Pexels page and was downloaded from Pexels under the Pexels License.
`rings-still` is a second crop of the same Pexels photo as `rings-ribbon` (id 32147285), used for anniversary still lifes.

Hero files are 4:3 and at most 250KB. Card files are 3:2 and at most 120KB. Each subject has a WebP and an AVIF.

## Gaps

- **One wrapped-gift frame** (Pexels 1303081, George Dolgikh) was dropped. The gifts sit in a corner of a large empty table, so a card crop looked blank.
- **Game consoles and branded electronics.** Stock frames were either a specific console logo or a close crop that would read as one model. Unmatched electronics titles, including `קונסולת משחקים`, use a wrapped-gift photo with the label `תמונה להמחשה`. They do not use a suitcase.
- **Jewelry.** One licensed still life (a gold necklace, no face). Wedding rings live in the wedding and anniversary sets. Extra jewelry frames were not added when they showed people or a specific retail model.
- **Bar and bat mitzvah.** Two frames without identifiable faces: a kiddush table and an open Torah scroll. Ceremony photos that showed identifiable people, including children, were not used.
- **Coffee.** The kitchen machine photo includes a small manufacturer plate. It is a category illustration, not the guest's exact model, and gift defaults carry `תמונה להמחשה`.
- The October 18 contact sheet and `e2e/fixtures/celebration/*` were style references only and are not shipped as product assets.
